# Negatives & Refuted Assumptions Ledger

Permanent log of **refuted claims, mathematical obstructions, negative controls, false assumptions, and engineering/toolchain traps** encountered in **Morpheus**.

> **Rule: Never delete negatives.** Keep every negative with its exact assumptions and obstruction. If a deeper model, weaker hypothesis, or newer toolchain later overturns a negative (or exposes a false assumption), update its **Status** and **Resolution** in place rather than erasing history.

## Status Legend

- `Active [GB]` — Established as a negative in the upstream Grok Bot baseline; pending independent `[X]` recheck.
- `Active [Verified]` — Independently confirmed in this repository/environment.
- `Scoped / Resolved` — Negative still holds under the original narrower hypothesis, while a stated relaxation resolves it.
- `Overturned` — Found to rest on a false assumption or bug (record date and correction).
